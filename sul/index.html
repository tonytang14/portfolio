SÜL

Overview

SÜL is developing an automated food-packaging system for pre-packaged meals, with the goal of improving the speed and consistency of meal assembly. I designed and prototyped three machine modules using 3D-printed parts, sheet metal, conveyor hardware, and other commercially available components. The system combined mechanical design with electrical controls to coordinate food dispensing, conveyor motion, and continuous bowl flow based on user input. Different approaches for gripping, dispensing, and ingredient handling were evaluated based on cost, throughput, flexibility, reliability, and mechanical complexity.
