SINTEF Ocean

Overview

In the summer of 2025, I participated in MIT's MISTI Norway program and interned at SINTEF Ocean, where I developed a simulation of the Reach Alpha 5 robotic arm for future integration with an underwater vehicle. The system is intended to support underwater inspection and repair tasks in aquaculture environments. Given a Cartesian target, the simulation uses inverse kinematics to determine the joint configuration required for the end effector to reach the desired position. I built the simulation in ROS 2 Humble using URDF models generated from SolidWorks and integrated RViz and MoveIt for visualization and motion planning. I also developed a custom ROS 2 node that accepts XYZ coordinates and commands the simulated arm to the target position.
